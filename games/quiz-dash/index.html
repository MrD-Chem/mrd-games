Run as far as you can

Your runner never stops. Dodge what is in your path and your score keeps climbing.

  • Score comes from distance, coins and quiz bonuses.
  • The game gets faster every 450m.
  • The scenery changes every 450m: snow, beach, night, then back to the meadow.
  • Your best score is saved on this device.

Move in three lanes

  • Left / Right (or A / D): change lane
  • Up, W or Space: jump
  • Down or S: duck
  • On a phone, swipe in any direction or use the buttons under the game.

Obstacles

  • Brown block: jump over it.
  • Red gate: duck under it.
  • Purple wall: switch lanes. Only a moon jump clears it.
  • Hitting one ends your run unless you have a shield.

Power-ups

  • Shield: absorbs one hit.
  • Slow-mo: slows the world for 6 seconds.
  • Double points: 8 seconds.
  • Moon jump: floaty, very high jumps for 8 seconds.

Checkpoint questions

Every 150m the game stops and asks a question.

  • Right answer: +150 points and you keep going.
  • Wrong answer: you are knocked back 50m, lose that much score and lose your shield.
