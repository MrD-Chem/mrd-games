Can this network run online races?

Open this page on a school Chromebook, on the school Wi-Fi. It tests each thing an online race needs, one at a time, and tells you in plain words if anything is blocked.

Checks

    The test signs in as an anonymous test player (no account, no names, no email), makes a hidden practice room, and deletes it again.

      Details for your IT department

      Online races in Mole Rush use Google Firebase over normal HTTPS (port 443). The web filter needs to allow these addresses:

      • identitytoolkit.googleapis.com and securetoken.googleapis.com (anonymous sign-in)
      • mr-d-chemistry-default-rtdb.firebaseio.com (the race database)
      • *.firebaseio.com (live updates can be redirected to another Firebase server)

      Live updates are a long-lived HTTPS stream (text/event-stream). A filter or proxy that holds back streaming responses until they end will make races freeze, even if the addresses are allowed.
